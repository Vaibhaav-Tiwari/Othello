# Othello Rules

This implementation uses an 8×8 board. Empty squares are available for play;
discs are either black (`B`) or white (`W`). The UI calls black **Noir** and
white **Blanc**.

## Starting position

`startBoard()` places four discs in the centre:

- `D4` and `E5` are white (Blanc).
- `E4` and `D5` are black (Noir).

The default turn is Noir. Board coordinates use columns **A–H** from left to
right and rows **1–8** from top to bottom, as generated by `moveName`.

## Legal moves and flips

`validMoves` checks every empty square. A move is legal only when `flipsFor`
finds at least one capture line from that square. In any of the eight
directions—horizontal, vertical, or diagonal—the line must contain one or
more consecutive opponent discs followed immediately by the moving player's
disc. A move may capture in several directions at once.

When a legal move is played, the new disc is placed and every opponent disc
found in all qualifying directions is flipped to the moving player's colour.
Occupied squares and moves with no such line are not legal.

### Opening example

At the initial position, Noir can play **D3**. Looking downward from D3 gives
Blanc's disc at D4 followed by Noir's disc at D5, so D3 is legal and **D4
flips from Blanc to Noir**. (There are four legal opening moves in total.)

## Passing and ending

After a move, the other player takes the turn. If that player has no legal
move, that player must pass and the opponent plays again. A pass is not
allowed when at least one legal move exists. If neither player has a legal
move, the game ends.

At the end, `countDiscs` counts the black and white discs on the board. The
player with more discs wins: the UI announces Noir or Blanc accordingly. If
the counts are equal, the result is a tie.
