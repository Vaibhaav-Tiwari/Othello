The exhibition

Sixty-four squares, two colours, endless reversals.

An artsy take on Othello (Reversi), hand-painted in a single HTML file — no build step, no dependencies, no image or audio assets. Play Noir against a minimax engine, or pass the board to a friend.

What's on the canvas

Features, in brief

◐

Full rules

Legal-move detection in all eight directions, disc flipping, forced passes and automatic game-over when neither side can move.

✧

A thinking engine

Minimax with alpha-beta pruning, move ordering that tries corners first, and an evaluation weighing position, corners, disc count and mobility.

⇄

Three modes

Play as Noir (black), as Blanc (white), or 2 Players locally. Choose which colour opens.

◉

Hints & help

Pulsing hint dots on legal squares (toggle with H), plus Hint a move, which asks the strongest engine for its pick.

↩

Chronicle

A running move list, undo, a pass button, and Copy PGN to export the game to your clipboard.

♪

Sound & light

Synthesised WebAudio tones, canvas particle bursts, 3D disc flips and a drifting aurora — each effect can be switched off.

How to play

The rules, gently

  1. Four discs open the board.

    Two black and two white sit crossed in the centre. Noir moves first by default.

  2. Surround to capture.

    Place a disc so that one or more straight lines of your opponent's discs — horizontal, vertical or diagonal — are bracketed between it and another of your discs. Every bracketed disc flips to your colour.

  3. Every move must flip.

    A move is only legal if it captures at least one disc. Gold dots mark your legal squares when hints are on.

  4. No move? Pass.

    If you have no legal move, your turn passes. The game won't let you pass while a legal move exists.

  5. Most discs wins.

    When neither player can move, the board is counted and the exhibition closes.

“Corners are gold. Edges are silver.”ATELIER NOTE

Controls

At your fingertips

On the board

Click a highlighted square to place a disc. The last move is framed in gold.

Keyboard

H toggles hint dots (when no dropdown or input is focused).

Buttons

Undo steps back to your last turn, New game resets, Pass move and Copy PGN live in the Chronicle.

Run it

Open, and play

Just open it

Double-click index.html. Everything is inline; only the Google Fonts need a network connection.

Local server

# from the repo root
python3 -m http.server 8000
# → http://localhost:8000/

Deploy to Netlify

Import the repo with an empty build command and publish directory ., or run:

netlify deploy --prod --dir .
